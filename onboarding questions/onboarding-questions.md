# Onboarding Questions

Questions asked during first-run onboarding, before the app pairs with the wearable.

## Basics
1. Male / Female
2. Age

## Sleep
3. What time do you go to sleep, and what time do you wake up?

## Lifestyle
4. What are your hobbies?
5. What does your diet consist of?
6. Do you smoke or drink?

## Weekly routine
7. What does Monday through Sunday look like for you — what are your activities
   throughout the week, and what is the routine?
